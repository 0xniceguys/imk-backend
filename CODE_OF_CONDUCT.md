# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want participation in the Immortal Kombat project to be open, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity, gender expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- being respectful in disagreement
- giving and receiving constructive feedback
- assuming good intent while staying precise about technical issues
- focusing critiques on the work, not the person
- showing empathy toward other contributors

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- insulting, demeaning, or inflammatory comments
- sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- publishing private information without permission
- sexualized language or attention
- bad-faith technical gatekeeping

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code of conduct. They may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this code of conduct.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to `shreyaan.work@gmail.com`. Include links, screenshots, and any relevant context. Reports will be reviewed as promptly as possible and handled with discretion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including warning contributors, removing content, temporarily restricting participation, or banning repeat offenders.
